Runs entirely in this page. Its Content Security Policy blocks all network requests, so nothing leaves your machine. Produces auth.json for contexter_frontend/public/.

Only the contexter_backend repo, permission Contents: Read and write, with an expiry date. Cleared from this page after encryption.

auth.json is public, so this password is all that protects the token. Use a long passphrase (at least 12 characters; 4+ random words is better).

At least 600,000. Higher is slower to brute-force but also slower to log in on your phone.
